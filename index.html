Why Axiom

Search got complicated. Answers didn’t.

Somewhere along the way, search engines started optimizing for everyone except the person searching. Axiom is what you get when you delete everything that isn’t the answer.

Answers first

The best result is an answer, not a scavenger hunt. Axiom reads the web so your first click is usually your last.

Private by default

No account required, no history tied to you, no ad-tech shadowing your queries. What you search is your business.

Zero ad clutter

Results can’t be bought. Nothing above the fold is sponsored, because nothing anywhere is sponsored.

Fast everywhere

A results page measured in kilobytes, not megabytes — built to feel instant on a phone in a subway, not just in a lab.

First principles

Our axioms

Most companies have values. We have axioms — five statements the whole product is derived from. When a feature contradicts one, the feature loses.

  1. The answer is the interface.

    Everything on the results page either moves you toward the answer or it doesn’t ship. There is no third category.

  2. Your attention belongs to you.

    We can’t sell what we refuse to collect. No auction runs behind your query, and no one bids on your eyeballs.

  3. Privacy is a default, not a setting.

    Protection you have to configure is protection most people never get. Axiom’s strictest mode is the one it ships in.

  4. Speed is respect.

    Every millisecond between question and answer is yours, not ours. We budget them like money.

  5. Rank is earned.

    No result can pay its way to the top. If a page can’t earn its position, it doesn’t get one.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is Axiom available yet?

Axiom is in private beta while we finish the public index. This site is where the launch happens — request early access and you’ll be first in line.

How does Axiom make money without ads?

Core search is free. A paid Axiom Pro tier will add power features — deeper research tools, API access, workspace features — for people who live in a search bar. We sell software, not searchers.

Does Axiom track my searches?

No. Queries are processed to serve your results, then aggregated or discarded. No advertising profile, no identity graph, no selling data. The details live on our privacy page.

Can I set Axiom as my browser’s default search engine?

Yes — at launch Axiom will plug into every major browser through the standard OpenSearch protocol (this site already ships the descriptor), so switching takes one click in your browser settings.

Why is it called Axiom?

An axiom is a truth so basic it needs no proof. We run the product the same way: five axioms, everything else derived from them. It also looks great with a dot on the i.
