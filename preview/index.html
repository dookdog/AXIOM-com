02 · TWO GATES, IN ORDER

Gate 1 is fatal. Gate 2 is corrective.

Most fact-checkers grade confidence. AXIOM checks order: leakage first, framing second. A claim that fails Gate 1 is dead on arrival — no amount of framing revives it.

Gate 1 — Leakage

If tomorrow leaked into today, nothing downstream can save the claim.

  • Lookahead
  • Overlap
  • Selection
  • Survivorship

Gate 2 — Framing

Survivors get reframed against the right baseline, costs, and denominators.

  • Benchmark
  • Regime
  • Cost
  • Multiplicity
  • Outliers
03 · THREE TIERS, NO PADDING

Every assertion lands in one of three places.

DOCUMENTED

A receipt exists and was checked. Locked in this demo — the demo checks nothing, so it awards nothing.

INFERRED

Reasoned from what is on the table, and labeled as reasoning.

NULL

Not found, not known, or fatally flagged — reported as exactly that. A scale that sometimes reads zero is the only scale you can trust when it reads full.
